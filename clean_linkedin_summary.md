(Hons) Electrical and Electronics Engineering Student I Engineeri... 4mo • Edited • Digital Dice (1—6) | EC4010 Digital Design Mini project I am happy to share my completed Digital Dice project for EC4010 Digital Design. This circuit behaves exactly like a real dice. Each time the user presses a button, a random number between 1 and 6 is generated and displayed on a 7-segment display. How the Circuit Works The system uses a high-speed counter approach to generate pseudo- random numbers. A 555 timer IC in astable mode generates a continuous clock signal at approximately 5 kHz. This clock drives a 4017 decade counter, which cycles through its outputs QO to Q5 sequentially. The 4017 counter is modified to reset after the 6th output by connecting Q6 to the Reset pin, limiting the range to exactly 1 through 6. A push button is connected to the Reset pin of the 555 timer. When pressed, the 555 oscillates and the counter runs. When released, the counter stops and the current value is captured and held. A diode matrix consisting of 9 x IN4148 diodes converts the 4017 outputs into the correct BCD codes (0001 to 0110). The BCD code is sent to a 7447 BCD to 7-segment decoder, which then drives a common anode 7-segment display to show the final number. Operating Sequence Power ON — The 7805 regulator provides stable 5V supply. Button pressed The 555 timer generates N5kHz clock pulses. The 4017 counter cycles through QO to Q5 at high speed, each number appearing for only 34.7 microseconds, which is faster than the human eye can perceive. Button
