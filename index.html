About Me & Engineering Philosophy

Bridging theoretical principles and physical implementation through electrical engineering, electronics, programming, and intelligent systems.

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Jaffna (Kilinochchi premises). My engineering ethos centers on developing dependable, practical, and data-driven solutions to real-world infrastructure and hardware challenges.

Throughout my undergraduate journey, I have cultivated hands-on capabilities across Power Systems, Renewable Energy Integration, Power Electronics, Control Systems, Analog & Digital Electronics, Custom PCB Prototyping, Embedded Firmware, and Enterprise Computer Networks.

Whether engineering an autonomous analog robot with zero microcontrollers, implementing a high-availability campus network with HSRP gateway redundancy, or designing residential electrical distribution systems to national (SLS 730) and international (IEC 60364) standards, I focus on safety, systematic testing, and measurable outcomes.
